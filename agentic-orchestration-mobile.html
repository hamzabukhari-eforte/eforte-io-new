Cross-functional expertise tailored for your needs

At the core of our AI delivery model are cross-functional AI Pods, designed to build, deploy, and scale industry-specific AI solutions.

Each Pod brings together AI Product Managers, AI Engineers, MLOps Engineers and Data Scientists, depending on the needs of the solution.

AI Product Manager

AI Product Managers envision the next generation of AI apps, unlocking value from novel ways of interaction with AI tools.

They follow a data-centric approach where data steers the capabilities of the product.

Data Scientist

A Data Scientist plays a central role across the AI lifecycle, combining technical, analytical, and strategic skills.

They act as strategist, experimenter, and ethical steward, ensuring AI systems are effective and safe.

AI Engineer

AI Engineers bridge the gap between data science and production software, focusing on deploying models and optimizing inference pipelines.

They work with large language models (LLMs), fine-tuning techniques, and integrating AI capabilities into robust architectures.

ML Ops Engineer

ML Ops Engineers establish infrastructure and processes for continuous integration, delivery, and deployment of machine learning models.

They ensure reproducibility, monitoring, and governance of AI systems throughout the lifecycle.
